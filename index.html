rclone Kwozi

rclone Kwozi is a personal OAuth client for rclone, the open-source command-line tool for cloud storage. Its only user is its owner, who uses it to upload the videos he makes for his channel, Kwozi Explains, from his own computer to a folder in his own Google Drive.

It is not a public service: there is no website to sign in to, no server, and no other users. rclone runs on the owner's machine and talks to Google Drive directly.
